At T2W Elite, we believe the coach is the most important part of any athlete's experience. A great coach can transform a player's development, confidence, and love for the game. A poor coach can do the opposite. This guide explains what we look for when recruiting coaches for T2W Elite Basketball.

## TEACHING ABILITY OVER BASKETBALL KNOWLEDGE

Basketball knowledge is important, but teaching ability matters more. A coach who knows the game but cannot communicate it to a 12-year-old is not effective. We look for coaches who can break down complex concepts into simple, age-appropriate instructions that athletes can understand and apply.

## WHAT WE VALUE

- Teaching: the ability to explain, demonstrate, and correct
- Character: integrity, patience, and consistency
- Development mindset: prioritizing long-term growth over short-term results
- Accountability: holding athletes to a standard with respect
- Communication: with athletes, parents, and other coaches
- Positivity: correcting without humiliating, pushing without breaking

## WHAT WE DO NOT WANT

- Coaches who rely on yelling and intimidation
- Coaches who prioritize winning over development at young ages
- Coaches who cannot communicate respectfully with parents
- Coaches who play favorites or give up on struggling players

## THE T2W COACHING STANDARD

T2W coaches do not lower the standard. We improve how the standard is taught. We hold athletes to high expectations and give them the tools, patience, and support to reach them. Our coaches are teachers first, competitors second, and role models always.

> FOR COACHES | If you are a coach who believes in teaching, development, and accountability, we want to talk to you. T2W Elite is always looking for coaches who can make a positive impact on young athletes.

## HOW TO APPLY

If you share our philosophy and want to coach with T2W Elite, we encourage you to apply. Coaching opportunities may include developmental teams, competitive teams, assistant coaching, skills training, camps, and future T2W sports divisions. Opportunities may require an interview, references, training, background screening, and agreement with T2W coaching and athlete-safety standards.

> KEY TAKEAWAY | T2W Elite looks for coaches who can teach, develop, and lead with character. We value teaching ability over basketball knowledge, a development mindset over winning at all costs, and positive accountability over intimidation. If you share this philosophy, we want you on our team.
