Turn Knowledge Into Teaching

The T2W Elite Coaches Corner helps coaches turn basketball knowledge into clear, age-appropriate teaching. Whether you are coaching your first youth team or preparing experienced athletes for higher levels of competition, these resources will help you organize practices, teach team concepts, communicate corrections, and develop players beyond the scoreboard.

The T2W Approach to Coaching
1

Teach Before You Criticize

Give athletes clear instructions they can understand and apply.

2

Correct the Action, Not the Athlete

Address the decision, technique, or behavior without attacking the player.

3

Develop the Complete Player

Build skills, decision-making, confidence, discipline, and leadership.

4

Progress With Purpose

Match drills, systems, and expectations to the athlete's developmental level.

5

Compete With Character

Create competitive teams that demonstrate effort, accountability, respect, and sportsmanship.

Corrections Players Can Actually Use

Connect. Identify. Correct. Rehearse. Reinforce.

Accountability does not require humiliation. Effective correction tells an athlete what happened, what needs to change, and what to do during the next opportunity.

INSTEAD OF

"Stop turning the ball over!"

TRY

Jump-stop, see the floor, and make the simple pass.

INSTEAD OF

"You're not playing defense!"

TRY

Get below the ball and keep your player and the ball in your vision.

INSTEAD OF

"Why didn't you box out?"

TRY

Make contact first, find the ball, and then pursue the rebound.

INSTEAD OF

"That was a terrible shot!"

TRY

On the next possession, check your balance, spacing, and the time remaining before you shoot.
